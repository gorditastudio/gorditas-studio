ABOUT ME

Edena Alvarado is an artist based in Chicago whose practice spans fine metals, steel, glass, and paper. Through metal sculpture and mixed-media collage, her work explores the preservation and memorialization of experiences surrounding childhood, memory, and grief. Her practice considers how personal and cultural histories can be preserved through material, form, and object-making.
